FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Call Our Help Desk
Do I need an appointment to visit a doctor?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking an appointment reduces your waiting time. Emergency patients are attended to immediately without an appointment.

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes. Our emergency department, pharmacy and ambulance service are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. We offer cashless treatment with leading insurance companies and TPAs. Please contact our insurance desk with your policy details for assistance.

How do I get my lab reports?

Reports can be collected from the lab counter, or shared on WhatsApp / email on request. Most routine reports are ready the same day.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking is available for patients and visitors within the premises.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Please call us at least 2 hours before your appointment time to reschedule or cancel.
